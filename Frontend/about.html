About SmartInvoice AI

AI-powered invoice tracking built for smarter expense management.

SmartInvoice AI helps users upload invoices, extract text with OCR, categorize expenses intelligently, visualize spending patterns, and gain AI-powered financial insights through a modern dashboard.

01

OCR Invoice Extraction

Automatically extracts invoice details and text from receipts, bills, and uploaded invoices using OCR technology.

02

AI Categorization

Smart classification of expenses into categories like Food, Travel, Shopping, Utilities, and more.

03

Analytics Dashboard

Interactive charts and monthly insights help users understand spending patterns and track expenses visually.

04

AI Recommendations

Receive intelligent suggestions and spending insights to improve budgeting and financial decision making.

Technology Stack

Built using modern frontend technologies and OCR tools for fast, responsive invoice processing.

Why SmartInvoice AI?

Manual invoice management is slow and error-prone. SmartInvoice AI simplifies the process by automating invoice extraction, organizing expenses, generating real-time analytics, and delivering AI-driven insights through a clean modern interface.
