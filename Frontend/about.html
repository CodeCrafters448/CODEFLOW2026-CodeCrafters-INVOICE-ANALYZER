About SmartInvoice AI

SmartInvoice AI is an OCR-powered intelligent invoice management platform. Automatically extract invoice details, categorize expenses, visualize spending patterns, and receive AI-powered financial insights.

Core Features

📄

OCR Invoice Extraction

Automatically extracts text and invoice details from uploaded receipts, bills, and invoices.

🤖

AI Expense Categorization

Intelligently categorizes expenses into Food, Travel, Shopping, Utilities, and more.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

Interactive charts and dashboards help track spending patterns and visualize your expenses.

💡

AI Recommendations

Personalized AI-driven insights to help users optimize their spending and financial decisions.

Technology Stack

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript Tesseract OCR Chart.js AI Analytics

Why SmartInvoice AI?

Managing invoices manually is time-consuming and error-prone. SmartInvoice AI automates invoice extraction, categorizes expenses, generates real-time analytics, and delivers AI-powered insights to help users track and optimize their financial health.
